Before you start

You need your PRISM email address and the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone.

Windows and Mac

  1. Download FortiClient VPN from the company portal.
  2. Open it and choose Configure VPN.
  3. Enter the server vpn.riverside-it.com and port 443.
  4. Sign in with your email, then approve the prompt on your phone.

iPhone

Install FortiClient from the App Store, then follow steps 2 to 4 above.

If the prompt never arrives, check that your phone has signal and try again after a minute.
