How to build it

  • Type a line: it's a step to tick.
  • Turn into a question (yes/no or pick one), something to write down, or a hidden password/code.
  • Only for hides a line, or a whole section, unless the device is on that MDM or of that type.
  • Under a yes/no question, indent lines (Tab) to show them only for that answer.
